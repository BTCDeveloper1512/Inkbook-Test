# StudioOS

StudioOS is a multi-tenant SaaS operating system for tattoo, piercing, and PMU studios. Each studio has an isolated workspace and a direct public booking page; StudioOS is not a public studio marketplace.

## User preferences

- German is the primary product and communication language.
- The brand should feel premium, calm, editorial, and emotionally focused on less administrative stress.
- Marketing must not use invented customer numbers, testimonials, logos, or unsupported claims.
